01The rules

1 minute of fees
→ 12 buys
→ burned.

These are the published rules of $TICK, in plain words. Nothing else happens to the fees.

  1. 0160s

    Claim

    The creator fees from $TICK are claimed every minute.

  2. 02÷12

    Split

    That minute of fees is split into 12 equal buys.

  3. 035s

    Buy

    One buy of $TICK every 5 seconds, all day.

  4. 04100%

    Burn

    Every token those buys get is burned.

Buys are 5 s apart while Solana blocks are about 0.2 s, so every buy lands on its own and you can watch each one arrive. A tick is one confirmed transaction ✓, and the tick of a clock.
